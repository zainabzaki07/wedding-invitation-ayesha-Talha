OUR STORY

A little of how it began

AUGUST 2021 · A CHANCE MEETING

A mutual friend's dinner, a spilled cup of chai, and an apology that turned into a two-hour conversation neither of them planned on.

"I only remember laughing. I don't remember being nervous at all."

MARCH 2023 · SAYING YES TO EACH OTHER

A quiet evening by the sea, a question asked without any rehearsed words, and an answer that came before he'd even finished it.

"Some love stories are loud. Ours was just certain."

JANUARY 2026 · THE FAMILIES MET

"That's when it stopped feeling like two lives, and started feeling like one."

FEBRUARY 2027 · AND NOW, THIS

Every ordinary Tuesday that led here, every small kindness — all of it was leading to the day we get to say it out loud.

"Come stand with us as we begin the rest of it."
